## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**: Genuinely product-specific in its signature moments, not a reskin. The timecode kicker ("00:04:00:00 — Casos de estudio"), the Reels-style muted-autoplay 9:16 preview cards with a single-active play/record toggle, and the frame-corner SVG marks on video cards are direct metaphors for a video editor's actual working environment — a generic consulting or SaaS portfolio wouldn't produce this vocabulary on its own. Where it slips toward interchangeable-agency territory: the stats bar, the accordion services list, and the polaroid-style testimonial cards are competent but generic patterns any freelancer site could reuse unchanged.

**Deterministic scan**: The CLI detector (`detect.mjs`) returned 43 findings on `index.html`: 33 `design-system-font-size` and 7 `design-system-color` (all advisory — literal values off the documented type/color ramp), plus one warning each for `em-dash-overuse` (39 em-dashes), `marquee` (the auto-scrolling client-logo strip), and one advisory `codex-grid-background`. The in-browser live-mode detector (a richer, separate rule set served at `/detect.js`) additionally caught real issues the CLI missed: `tiny-text` (11px `.hero-index`), `undersized-ui-text` (10px `.placeholder-label`, below an 11px floor), `tight-leading` (1.18 line-height on `.audience-lead`), and `wide-tracking` on testimonial captions.

**False positives worth naming explicitly**: the browser detector's `ai-color-palette` flag (fired on the amethyst accent and its gradients) and `dark-glow` flag (fired on the amethyst box-shadows) are both the site's deliberately pinned, brief-confirmed palette and its documented "Regla de la Sombra Tibia" warm-shadow rule — not generic AI-purple slop. Likewise `all-caps-body` on kickers/labels is the confirmed Label typography role, not accidental. `codex-grid-background` correctly targets the hero viewfinder grid (`.hero-grid`); it's a deliberate rule-of-thirds/viewfinder device tied to the cinematic direction, kept intentionally per DESIGN.md despite the detector's default suspicion of grid overlays. `repeating-stripes-gradient` on the testimonial photo placeholder and the plain `em-dash-overuse`/`marquee` findings are pre-existing from before this redesign pass and were out of scope for it — still valid to fix, just not new regressions.

## Overall Impression

The redesign's signature devices — timecodes, the film-grain/vignette treatment, the Reels-style video cards — are specific, well-executed, and genuinely tied to what Dai does for a living; the aesthetic execution (heuristic #8, scored 4/4) is the strongest part of the site. But the site's proof section is currently working against itself: real client names are hidden from screen readers inside an `aria-hidden` marquee, the flagship video-play interaction is a dead control site-wide, and the placeholder case-study grid lands at the exact moment a skeptical visitor needs the strongest evidence. The biggest opportunity is closing the gap between the interaction's promise (styled, hover-scaled, ARIA-labeled as a button) and its current behavior (nothing happens) before any of the visual polish matters more.

## What's Working

- **The timecode kicker system** ("00:0N:00:00 — Nombre") ties navigation, section identity, and Dai's actual craft into one load-bearing device — it works because it's literal to the job, not decorative.
- **Mono-accent discipline**: amethyst is the only functional color anywhere on the site; olive/moss stay confined to atmospheric background washes. The restraint reads immediately and gives the piece a coherent, non-generic mood — this is what both assessments independently rated as the standout quality (Aesthetic heuristic: 4/4).
- **Services accordion** is textbook progressive disclosure — single-open at a time, smooth `grid-template-rows: 0fr → 1fr` collapse, arrow rotation — a clean, well-executed micro-interaction with no rough edges.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] Video play button is a dead, unreachable control**
Why it matters: this is the site's signature interaction on its most important section (project proof). The `.work-frame .play` icon has `cursor:pointer` and a hover-scale on the whole card, but the JS returns early before attaching any click listener when `data-src` is empty — clicking it, which the styling actively invites, produces total silence. It also has no `tabindex`, so a keyboard user can't reach a control whose `role="button"` and `aria-label="Reproducir con sonido"` both claim it's operable — a direct role/behavior mismatch.
Fix: give the toggle `tabindex="0"` plus a `keydown` handler unconditionally; when no real video exists yet, either strip the interactive affordance (role, aria-label, cursor) or surface a visible "Video próximamente" state instead of silent nothing.
Suggested command: **$impeccable harden**

**[P1] Real client proof is invisible to screen reader users**
Why it matters: `.marquee-track` — which contains real, PRODUCT.md-confirmed clients (Hongos Porto, Café Martínez, Brahma, Roche, Oregon Hotel, Tun) — is wrapped entirely in `aria-hidden="true"`. Unlike the genuinely-placeholder case studies and testimonials, this is real social proof on a Persuade surface, and it's completely inaudible to assistive tech.
Fix: keep `aria-hidden` only on the marquee's duplicated loop copy (needed for the seamless-scroll illusion), and add one non-hidden, visually-hidden list of the unique client names before it.
Suggested command: **$impeccable harden**

**[P2] Hero copy conflicts with the site's own solo-freelancer principle**
Why it matters: `.hero-index` reads "Estudio de contenido" (studio), directly in tension with PRODUCT.md's stated principle that positioning must never imply a team that doesn't exist — this is a documented product-truth conflict, not a stylistic nitpick.
Fix: reword to keep the cinematic register without implying a studio/team.
Suggested command: **$impeccable clarify**

**[P2] Footer text and video metrics fall below AA contrast/size floors**
Why it matters: `.footer-fine` (11px, opacity .4 on `--noir`) computes to ~3.31:1 — below the 4.5:1 AA minimum — right next to a CSS comment noting other low-opacity text was already contrast-checked in an earlier harden pass, suggesting this one slipped through. The browser detector independently flagged the same family of issue elsewhere: 11px `.hero-index` (`tiny-text`) and 10px `.placeholder-label` (`undersized-ui-text`, below an 11px floor).
Fix: raise `.footer-fine` opacity to ≥.55 or switch to a solid muted color token; bump `.placeholder-label` to ≥11px.
Suggested command: **$impeccable harden**

**[P2] "Casos de estudio" placeholder state lands at the site's peak trust moment**
Why it matters: six identical placeholder cards ("Nombre del proyecto / Cliente — a definir") sit immediately after the stats section ("100+ proyectos realizados"), creating the sharpest emotional valley in the whole scroll at the exact point a skeptical visitor needs the strongest proof, with only one small `.work-note` line acknowledging the placeholder state.
Fix: differentiate the placeholder framing more intentionally — stronger "en camino" treatment, or fewer, more editorially-framed placeholder cards instead of six identical repeats.
Suggested command: **$impeccable clarify**

## Minor Observations

- Site nav never indicates the active/current section as the user scrolls — only the timecode kicker signals location, and only if read.
- Contact form success copy overclaims transmission ("recibí tu mensaje") despite no backend being wired — worth revisiting even though the no-backend state itself is intentional.
- Brand marquee auto-scrolls continuously and can only be paused via mouse hover — no keyboard/touch pause mechanism (though it's fully neutralized under `prefers-reduced-motion`).
- `.audience-lead` line-height (1.18) is tight for a multi-line serif pull-quote; the browser detector flagged this independently (`tight-leading`).
- `nav-toggle` keeps the static `aria-label="Abrir menú"` even once the drawer is open — only `aria-expanded` updates; "Cerrar menú" would match state more precisely.
- `em-dash-overuse` (39 instances) and the auto-scrolling `marquee` pattern are both pre-existing from before this redesign pass and were out of scope for it, but remain open findings.
